# iCog Labs

iCog Labs is an artificial intelligence research and software development company
based in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia. It was founded in 2013 by Getnet Aseffa together
with Ben Goertzel. iCog Labs is one of the most prominent AI companies in East
Africa and a key contributor to open-source artificial general intelligence (AGI)
research.

## Mission and focus

iCog Labs focuses on building toward Artificial General Intelligence (AGI). The
company contributes engineering effort to the OpenCog and OpenCog Hyperon
frameworks and develops tools, language implementations, and applications on top
of them. Much of its work is carried out in the MeTTa programming language and the
AtomSpace knowledge representation system.

## Relationship to other organizations

iCog Labs collaborates closely with SingularityNET and TrueAGI on AGI research.
Ben Goertzel, who co-founded iCog Labs, is also the founder of SingularityNET and
the lead architect behind the OpenCog and Hyperon projects. Through these ties,
iCog Labs engineers contribute to the broader OpenCog ecosystem maintained under
the `trueagi-io` and `opencog` organizations on GitHub.

## Community programs

iCog Labs runs internship and training programs for Ethiopian software engineers
and students, including training on advanced topics such as Retrieval-Augmented
Generation (RAG), knowledge graphs, and the MeTTa language. The "Anyone Can Code"
(ACC) initiative, supported by iCog Labs, introduces programming and computational
thinking to students across Africa.

## Projects developed or contributed to by iCog Labs

iCog Labs engineers contribute to several projects in the OpenCog Hyperon
ecosystem, including:

- **metta-moses** - a reimplementation of the MOSES program-learning algorithm in MeTTaLog.
- **bio-semantic-parser** - a biomedical text-to-knowledge parser.
- **MeTTaLog (metta-wam)** - a MeTTa implementation running on the Warren Abstract Machine.
- Tooling and applications built on the **AtomSpace**.

iCog Labs is headquartered in Addis Ababa and uses the AtomSpace as a core
knowledge-representation technology across many of its projects.
