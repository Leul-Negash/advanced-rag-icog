# SingularityNET and TrueAGI

SingularityNET is a decentralized artificial intelligence platform and marketplace
founded in 2017 by **Ben Goertzel**. Its goal is to create a decentralized,
democratic, and beneficial Artificial General Intelligence. SingularityNET is the
main organizational and funding home of the **OpenCog Hyperon** project.

## Relationship to OpenCog Hyperon

SingularityNET coordinates and funds much of the OpenCog Hyperon development. Ben
Goertzel, the founder of SingularityNET, is also the chief scientific architect of
Hyperon. The Hyperon codebase is hosted largely under the **trueagi-io** GitHub
organization, which is associated with SingularityNET's AGI efforts.

## TrueAGI

TrueAGI is the organization (and GitHub org `trueagi-io`) under which much of the
Hyperon ecosystem is developed, including MeTTaLog (metta-wam), MORK, and PeTTa.
TrueAGI is closely tied to SingularityNET's mission of building beneficial AGI.

## The broader ecosystem

The AGI ecosystem connected to SingularityNET includes:

- **OpenCog Hyperon** - the AGI framework.
- **iCog Labs** - an engineering partner in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia.
- **TrueAGI / trueagi-io** - the home of much Hyperon code.
- **OpenCog Foundation** - the steward of the original OpenCog project.

## Relationship to other components

- SingularityNET **funds and coordinates** OpenCog Hyperon.
- Ben Goertzel **founded** SingularityNET and **co-founded** iCog Labs.
- SingularityNET **collaborates with** iCog Labs on AGI research.
- TrueAGI (`trueagi-io`) **hosts** MeTTaLog, MORK, and PeTTa.
